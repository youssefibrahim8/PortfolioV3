The Butterfly Effect

A hackathon tool where residents describe a problem in their neighbourhood and an AI agent links it to evidence-backed fixes for heat, health and housing, keeping sourced facts apart from its own guesses.

Runs on a Naive Bayes classifier I wrote from scratch: it trains in the browser, scores 84% on held-out tests and needs no outside AI service.
